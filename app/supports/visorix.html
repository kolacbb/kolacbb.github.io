Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use an old device as a camera?

Open Visorix on your old device and enable "Camera Mode." It will generate a local IP address or QR code. Use Visorix on your primary device to scan or enter the address to start streaming.

Supported camera types?

Visorix supports most IP cameras that follow the RTSP or ONVIF standards, as well as peer-to-peer WebRTC connections.

Why can't I see my device?

Ensure both devices are connected to the same Wi-Fi network and that you have granted "Local Network" permissions in your iOS Settings.
